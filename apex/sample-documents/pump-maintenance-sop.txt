Pump Maintenance Procedure (synthetic sample)

Before any pump repair, isolate electrical power and apply lockout tagout. Confirm zero energy with the site electrician before opening the casing.

Routine pump maintenance must be scheduled during the weekly maintenance window on Tuesday between 06:00 and 08:00. Emergency repairs may be done outside the window with supervisor approval.

After repair, record the work order number, parts replaced, and the technician name in the maintenance register.
